ASDAC brings together national soil institutes, universities and research institutes to optimize the soil data infrastructure in Africa.

## How ASDAC organizes Soil Data

Soil data, contributed by its members, is aggregated at a continental level to predict soil health status at continental level as accurate as possible. In order to facilitate the aggregation process, guidance is made available to members on how to describe the data with a rich context. This guidence expects the FAIR principles as a core and builds on it using conventions from the RO-Crate community.
