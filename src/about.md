## Who is AUSO?

Soil information is critical for managing agriculture and the environment in Africa. The rapid decline in soil health on the continent threatens food security, economic development, and resilience to climate change. The current state of soil degradation poses a significant threat to African biodiversity and ecosystems, contributing directly to reduced soil productivity and increased vulnerability.

Prompt action to provide accurate, site-specific soil data will enable tailored technical, policy, and infrastructural interventions to inform soil management, which will improve productivity, reduce environmental degradation, and support sustainable livelihoods.

The Africa Union Soil Observatory (AUSO) facilitates the collection and use of soil information for decision making at all levels.

## About the AUSO project

AUSO is been initiated as part of a European Commission project for Developing African Union Soil Observatory (AUSO) [Project: 101218840 — AUSO — HORIZON-MISS-2024-SOIL-01](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101218840).

The main objective of AUSO is to establish a comprehensive soil information system for Africa to support the management of African soils. The project’s goals include:

- Developing a scalable soil health dashboard for Africa, along with a plan for long-term sustainability;
- Identifying significant local and regional thresholds for essential soil health descriptors;
- Collecting new data strategically to address knowledge gaps in areas such as pollution, compaction, biodiversity, nutrient status, acidity and acidification, soil organic matter, erosion, and water content, thereby improving the dashboard’s accuracy and utility through consultation with national stakeholders;
- Creating roadmaps to illustrate trends in soil health responses to policy instruments; and
- Supporting participating African countries in developing national soil health strategies, managing soil resources for agriculture and environmental purposes, establishing use cases, and providing training on data interpretation and application. The project aligns with continental initiatives such as the Soil Initiative for Africa (SIA) and the Africa Fertilizer and Soil Health Action Plan (AFSH-AP).

AUSO is being implemented by a consortium of 23 African and European organizations. It received funding from the Horizon Europe – Research and Innovation program and aligns with the EC Soil Mission program.

## What is ASDAC

Soil data, contributed by its members, is aggregated at a continental level to predict soil health status at continental level as accurate as possible. The African Soil Data Center hosted at [FARA](https://faraafrica.org/), is a body which orchestrates the various datasets contributed by memberstates in combination with commonly available data into continental soil information products. In order to facilitate the data selection and aggregation process, guidance is made available to members on how to describe the data with a rich standardised context. This guidance builds on the [FAIR principles](https://www.gofair.foundation/fair-principles).
