African Union Soil Observatory

Building a Collaborative Foundation for Sustainable Agriculture

Aggregating soil data from research institutions across Africa to support sustainable land management

🗺️

Interactive Mapping

Explore soil types, properties, and characteristics across African nations with our advanced map viewer. Zoom in to view regional and local soil data.

📊

Data Aggregation

Access datasets from leading African soil research institutions. Harmonized data on soil texture, pH, carbon content, and more.

👥

Citizen Science

Contribute your own soil observations. Build crowdsourced datasets that strengthen our understanding of African soils.

📈

Real-Time Monitoring

Track temporal changes in soil properties through observation networks and sensor data across key agricultural regions.

🌱

Agricultural Insights

Inform crop selection, irrigation planning, and fertility management with evidence-based soil information.

🔬

Research Access

Support academic research with freely available, published soil datasets under open licenses.
